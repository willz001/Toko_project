ABOUT WAKAGO

Connecting Yola with local talent.

Wakago helps people discover local service providers in Yola, Adamawa State, and contact them directly.

⌖

Our mission

Make it easier to find local shops and skilled service providers without needing an account or submitting a request through a separate platform.

🤝

How it works

Choose a category, filter vendors by neighbourhood, view their profile, check the listed rating and use the WhatsApp or phone link to contact them.

📍

Starting in Yola

The prototype focuses on areas such as Bekaji, Karewa, Jimeta Ultra Modern Market, Shopping Complex and Paluja.
